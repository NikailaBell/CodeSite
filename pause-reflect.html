Pause & Reflect

A Moment for Deeper Consideration

Take these one step at a time. Consider how our assumptions affect real lives before designing a response.

1. Program Assumptions
2. Materials vs. Governance
3. Your Context
Question 1 of 3

Think of a shelter program you know about. What constraint did it assume? Did anyone actually diagnose whether that was the binding constraint?

💡

Take a quiet moment to picture the program, examine its core premise, and consider what evidence was relied upon.

Question 2 of 3

If control and governance are often binding constraints in shelter responses, why do programs focus so heavily on materials and construction?

💡

Reflect on organizational incentives, visibility, and how easy it is to measure physical output versus political reality.

Question 3 of 3

How would you diagnose constraints in your own context? What questions would you ask? Who would you talk to?

💡

Consider who holds the actual answers in your setting and how you can ensure their voices guide your decisions.

✔

Reflection Complete

Thank you for taking a moment to pause, examine your assumptions, and center human reality in your thinking.
