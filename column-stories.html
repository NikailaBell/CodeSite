Case Profiles — Column Layout

A responsive, color-coded grid perfect for comparing different user personas, scenarios, or case studies side-by-side.

🛠️ How to use 3 vs. 4 Columns

This layout uses Flexbox to automatically size the columns to fit on one row. The template below includes 3 columns by default.

If you need a 4th story, simply duplicate one of the <section class="story-card..."> blocks and change its color class to story-col4 to apply the purple hue (#F5F0FF).

Live Preview

Tent 1: The Water Merchant

Ibrahim salvaged ten 200-liter drums from his old hardware store. "Control water, control everything," he explains. By waking at 3 AM to fill his drums, he bypasses the daytime crowds. By 7 AM, he sells the water to working families at triple the effort cost.

Tent 2: The Daily Math

Fatima's morning starts with impossible calculations. "If I queue for water, I lose four hours of work time. Four hours equals 200 shillings lost." She shows a small notebook detailing her survival trade-offs: Water vs. food. Safety vs. access. Health vs. hunger.

Tent 3: Beyond Coping

Grace is 73. Her grandson, Samuel, has cerebral palsy, and his wheelchair broke during their evacuation. Grace's arthritis makes carrying heavy water containers impossible. "They announce 'vulnerable priority' at distributions," Grace sighs. "But priority means nothing in a crowd."
